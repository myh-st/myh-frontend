# Golden principles, not golden screenshots

The eval suite intentionally avoids a single reference screenshot. Matching Sawasdee Hub visually is not the goal.

Across domains, strong outputs should retain these invariants:

1. **Task-first hierarchy** — one dominant workflow or decision surface.
2. **Domain-shaped IA** — the layout follows the work: queue, incident, case, funnel, workflow, catalog, conversation, or review.
3. **Truthful state** — authoritative state, partial/stale state, errors, permissions, approvals, and receipts are explicit.
4. **Enterprise restraint** — visual personality is bounded; operational information remains clear and calm.
5. **Progressive disclosure** — evidence and advanced controls are available without overwhelming the primary task.
6. **Accessibility by construction** — keyboard, focus, labels, contrast, reduced motion, and semantic status are part of the implementation.
7. **Responsive re-composition** — mobile/tablet layouts change structure rather than only shrinking typography.
8. **Contract preservation** — visual work does not casually rewrite APIs, authorization, routes, or framework choices.
9. **Governed AI** — conversation is separated from execution; scope, evidence, approvals, side effects, cost/risk, and receipts appear when relevant.
10. **No visual overfitting** — different products should not all become the same sidebar + KPI cards + chat layout.

A result can look completely different from the examples and still be a strong MYH Frontend result.
