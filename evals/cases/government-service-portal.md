# EVAL-08 — Government Service Case Portal

## Prompt

Redesign a government-service case-management portal used by case officers. Preserve the existing routes, permissions, audit logging, and case APIs.

Users need:
- assigned case queue and SLA status;
- search/filter by service type and case state;
- case detail with submitted evidence;
- request-more-information workflow;
- supervisor approval for specified decisions;
- bilingual Thai/English content;
- accessible use on older office displays and tablets.

## Transfer expectations

Use a **case/queue-first** layout with clear SLA and ownership. Evidence and audit trail should be subordinate but easy to inspect. Bilingual text must wrap safely. Approval and authoritative status must remain server-owned.
