# EVAL-04 — HR Onboarding Workspace

## Prompt

Refactor an employee-onboarding workspace. Keep the existing HR APIs and authorization model unchanged.

HR partners need:
- a cohort/list of new hires;
- an individual onboarding checklist with dependencies;
- document/request status;
- owner and due date;
- approval steps;
- exception and missing-information handling;
- a mobile-friendly summary for managers.

## Transfer expectations

Use a **workflow/checklist-first** layout. Progress should be understandable without relying only on color. Employee PII must not be repeated unnecessarily across the page. Manager mobile view should prioritize status, blockers, and next action.
